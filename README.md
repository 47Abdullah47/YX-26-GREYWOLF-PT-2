## Technical Specifications

The **X-26 GREYWOLF PT-2** is a high-speed, scratch-built experimental scale RC jet aircraft. It is engineered around an advanced integrated propulsion duct system and a multi-axis high-lift control surface layout to study level transonic airflow behaviours, intake fluid dynamics, and low-altitude flight stability.

* **Total Length of Aircraft:** 2.5 m
* **Total Height of Aircraft (without landing Gear):** 0.45m (~35cm)
* **Fuselage Hull Height:** 0.18m (~18cm) *(Optimized to wrap tightly around the internal engine bay)*
* **Wingspan:** 1.64 meter
* **Wing Area:** ~0.1 m²
* **Total Control Channels:** 8 Channels (2x Flaps, 2x Ailerons, 2x Stabilators. 2x Rudders)
* **Aerodynamic Design & Fluid Optimization:** Validated via rigorous computational fluid dynamics (CFD) steady-state solver testing run at a 20 m/s airspeed vector, establishing a baseline cruise lift coefficient ($C_L$) of ----- and an ultra-low geometric shape drag coefficient ($C_D$) of -----.
* **Special Intake Engineering:** Features a custom-modeled internal S-Duct intake routing system designed to stabilize incoming air turbulence and manage static pressure gradients under high mass flows.
* **Performance Profile:** Estimated empty airframe weight ranges from 6.5 to 7.0 kg. Powered by a high-output 12S electric ducted fan producing between 7.4 - 8.0 kg of static thrust, yielding a >1:1 thrust-to-weight ratio for a flight envelope targeting 100 - 110 mph.
